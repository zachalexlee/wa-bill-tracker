Bill Summary

HB 1001 authorizes capital projects for fire protection services across Washington State. This is a standard capital budget appropriation bill that funds construction, renovation, and equipment acquisition for fire districts and fire protection authorities.

Key Provisions
  • Capital appropriations for fire station construction/renovation
  • Equipment acquisition (apparatus, communications)
  • Grants to local fire districts
  • Bond financing authorization
Fiscal Impact

State: Bond issuance + general fund appropriations. Exact amount TBD in committee.
Local: Matching requirements for districts receiving grants.
Private Sector: Construction contracts bid out; opportunity for contractors.

Stakeholder Positions (Detailed)
  • Washington State Fire Fighters Union Strong Support
  • Association of Washington Cities Support
  • Washington State Association of Counties Support
  • Building Industry Association of WA Support (contracts)
  • WA Policy Center (fiscal conservative) Oppose (spending)
Political Dynamics
